# Neon industrial building pass

Reference: owner-supplied cyberpunk tower image, 20 September 2026. Used for architectural direction (layered massing, exposed services, warm occupied spaces and framed advertising), not as a pasted façade or a claim of matching its photorealism. Existing original billboard atlas reused; no third-party logos, purchased assets, added downloads or generated media.

Implementation: `src/game/NeonArchitecture.ts` adds shared procedural room artwork behind projecting mullions, floor beams, dark window recesses, shutters, service decks with connected rails, cylindrical utility risers with collars and ventilation housings. Details wrap the front and both sides of 88 street buildings plus the main boulevard landmark. `NeonEnvironment.ts` adds selected offset crowns and thick billboard frames, replacing the old front balcony/air-conditioning detail. The existing shop interiors, road, vehicle and lighting/post pipeline are retained.

Review iterations: the first pass improved fronts but left the prominently visible sides flat. Extended the kit around corners. The second pass made the repeated lit rooms too bright and schematic; reduced wall luminance, added curtains/blinds and edge shading, and kept most side rooms dark. Four chase views and a narrow mobile cockpit view were inspected. Current architecture remains procedural and modular, not the reference image's film-quality asset density.

Evidence: `artifacts/neon/architecture/` contains before, first-pass, final and reviewed screenshots plus renderer JSON. Desktop 1440×900 high quality at lap fractions .02, .18, .49 and .76; mobile 390×844 cockpit. No browser page/console errors in desktop checks. Production TypeScript/Vite build passed. Movement checked using real keyboard input and physics telemetry.

Cost: new kit has 11 instanced batches, three shared geometries and four 512×320 room textures. No additional dynamic lights, shadow casters, post passes or animation update. Frame totals (including existing rendering passes) rose from approximately 1.00–1.05 million to 1.17–1.22 million triangles across matched sample locations; draw calls from 267–396 to 284–416. Total texture count +4 and geometry count +3. Measurements vary with moving rivals and existing scene culling; this is not an FPS equivalence claim or a hardware benchmark. All kit geometry/materials remain under the environment for normal scene disposal.
